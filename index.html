You can vouch after a trade is done. You can report scammers in my discord. If you have proof ill ban them personally.

The commands

  • /trade list — put something up for sale. The bot checks it against the Deepwoken Wiki first so you can't list fake items.
  • /trade find — search for an item across every server the bot is in.
  • /trade profile — check someone's vouch count and what they're selling.
  • /trade mylistings — see your own active listings.
  • /trade remove — take a listing down once you've sold it.
  • /trade complete — vouch for someone after a trade.
  • /trade help — see all this in Discord, in case you forget.
